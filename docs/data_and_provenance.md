# Local data and provenance

CellState is local-first. Installation, CI, and examples do not contact GEO, CELLxGENE, or any other data server. The supported public-data workflow is to obtain a dataset under its stated access terms yourself, inspect its license/consent and preprocessing, and point CellState at its local `.h5ad`, 10x `.h5`, or Matrix Market directory.

For reproducibility, preserve the original accession, source URL, retrieval date, license/access terms, feature identifier convention, sample/donor mapping, and preprocessing history in a separate study record. The `load_dataset` API accepts `source_url` and `license_terms`; its manifest records local path and SHA-256. A checksum does not establish legal permission to use or redistribute data.

Do not commit raw controlled-access data, credentials, or identifying metadata. Synthetic files generated by the examples are clearly labeled and are safe for automated tests; they must not be represented as real biological observations.
